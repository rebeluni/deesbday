## 🎯 DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Color Theory Applied
- **Complementary**: Pink + Cyan (opposite on color wheel)
- **Analogous**: Pink + Purple + Cyan (adjacent colors)
- **Triadic**: Pink + Purple + Gold (evenly spaced)
- **Saturation**: Vibrant but not oversaturated
- **Value**: Good contrast between light and dark

### Visual Hierarchy
1. **Most Prominent**: Deep Pink (#FF006E)
2. **Secondary**: Purple (#B537F2) and Cyan (#00B4D8)
3. **Accents**: Gold (#FFB703) and Green (#06FFA5)
